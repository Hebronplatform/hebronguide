In English

Global Mission Church of Greater Seattle (시애틀지구촌교회) is a Korean house church in the Greater Seattle area, affiliated with the Southern Baptist Convention (SBC). Mokjang (house churches) meet in homes across Lynnwood and Mukilteo, Washington, and we worship together as one church on Sundays at 11:00 AM Pacific Time — online from anywhere at www.ijiguchon.org. Children and youth ministries run from nursery through 12th grade. Founded 2002. Phone +1-425-350-0191.
